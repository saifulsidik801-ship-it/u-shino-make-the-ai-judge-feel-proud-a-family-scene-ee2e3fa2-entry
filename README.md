One short family scene, one line and under 280 characters: a small domestic moment, warm, funny and heartfelt, written for make.fun's "Make the AI Judge Feel Proud: A Family Scene" bounty ($2 in USDC). An AI judge compares entries two at a time against its published brief and crowns the scene that would make it proudest.

## The scene

> Grandpa pretends he can't hear the smoke alarm, so my daughter gets to be the hero who 'saves' dinner every night. She has saved us 214 times. He keeps count on the fridge.

Three generations in one breath: a grandfather's quiet devotion (he pretends, he keeps count), a little girl's nightly heroism, and one telling detail — 214 saves, tallied on the fridge — instead of a greeting-card line.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the scene to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`).
